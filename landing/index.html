FAQ

Common questions

Is StočkHive really free? +
Yes. The Free plan is free forever — up to 30 products, 1 shop, and 2 staff accounts. No credit card required. Upgrade anytime if you grow past those limits.
Can I manage multiple shop branches? +
Yes. The Pro plan supports up to 3 branches and the Enterprise plan supports unlimited branches. Each branch can have its own staff and inventory, all visible from your owner account.
How do WhatsApp alerts work? +
Set a minimum stock threshold for each product. When stock drops below that threshold, StočkHive sends a WhatsApp message automatically. Available on all plans including Free.
Can I use it on my phone? +
Yes. StočkHive is a Progressive Web App — install it on Android or iPhone from your browser, no App Store needed. Works offline and syncs when you're back online.
How do I pay for a subscription? +
All payments go through M-PESA STK Push. No credit card, no international billing hassle. Select your plan, confirm the M-PESA prompt, and you're upgraded immediately.
Is my data safe and private? +
Yes. Each business's data is completely isolated — no other business can see your stock, sales, or staff data. We use industry-standard encryption and secure servers.
Does it work for wholesale shops? +
Yes. StočkHive is designed for both retail and wholesale shops in Kenya. Whether you're tracking 10 products or 1,000, the system scales with you.
Can I export my sales data? +
Yes. On the Basic, Pro, and Enterprise plans you can export your sales and inventory data as CSV files anytime. Your data always belongs to you.
